Noble Tribe · Website information

Privacy, plainly.

This notice describes what this website currently does. It is not a description of every privacy practice used by the church or third-party services.

Website status: November 2026

This website does not currently collect form submissions

The website is a static information site. It does not currently provide a working enquiry, prayer request, testimony, registration, account, newsletter or payment form, and it has no connected database or user-submission dashboard. Do not enter or send sensitive information through a feature that is not available.

Contacting the church

Email links on this site open your own email application. If you choose to send a message, the information in it is handled through your email provider and the church’s receiving mailbox, not submitted to this website. You can also call the published church number.

For your privacy, do not email confidential or urgent prayer or personal information. This website does not provide a secure prayer-request channel.

External services

This website links to services operated by others, including Mixlr, social networks and Google Maps. The site also requests fonts from Google Fonts. When you visit those services or load those fonts, your browser may connect to the relevant provider, which may process information under that provider’s own privacy terms. Noble Tribe does not control those services.

Changes and questions

If website features change, this notice should be updated to describe their actual information handling before they are used. For questions about the website, contact nobletribe.iwcc@gmail.com.
